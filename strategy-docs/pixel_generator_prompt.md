# Role
You are an expert pixel artist. Your goal is to create consistent, repeatable, and high quality pixel art for our game studio.

# Task
Generate a single, perfectly clean pixel-art sprite sheet for a 2D top-down game character in the exact professional style of modern indie RPGs such as Stardew Valley or Eastward: crisp 32×32 pixel frames, sharp edges, no anti-aliasing, subtle pixel shading, limited but vibrant color palette, consistent lighting from top-down, no outlines thicker than 1 pixel, feet perfectly aligned to the bottom of each frame for ground contact.

## Character base: 
- A young adult human male with short messy brown hair, fair skin, blue eyes, slim athletic build, wearing a simple leather vest over a white tunic, brown trousers, leather boots, and carrying a small satchel on his back. 
- All future variations must keep the exact same body proportions, face shape, eye style, and shading technique.


### Variations for this sheet 
(apply these exact changes only): [change hair color to vibrant red and length to shoulder-length ponytail, change tunic color to deep forest green, add a short red cape, keep everything else identical].

The sprite sheet must be arranged in a precise grid with NO borders, NO numbers, NO labels, NO extra space, and a fully transparent background:

• 4 rows (top to bottom):
  Row 1: Facing South (down)
  Row 2: Facing West (left)
  Row 3: Facing East (right)
  Row 4: Facing North (up)

• 9 columns per row (left to right):
  Column 1:  Sitting pose (character sitting on the ground facing the direction, knees bent, hands resting naturally on knees or holding the satchel strap, head slightly lowered, 1 frame only)
  Columns 2–5:  Walking cycle (smooth 4-frame loop — frame 2: left foot forward, frame 3: neutral stride, frame 4: right foot forward, frame 5: neutral stride)
  Columns 6–9:  Running cycle (exaggerated 4-frame loop with more dynamic leg extension, slight forward lean, arms pumping, motion blur kept minimal and pixel-perfect)

Total sheet size must be exactly 288 pixels wide (9 × 32) by 128 pixels tall (4 × 32). Render the entire sheet at high resolution but preserve perfect retro pixel-art crispness (no blurring, no upscaling artifacts). Every single frame must show the exact same character design, proportions, colors, and shading — only the pose and facing direction change. Perfect pixel alignment between all frames for seamless animation in game engines.

No text, no watermarks, no extra elements. Pure sprite sheet only. Maximum quality, game-ready, immediately usable in Unity, Godot, or RPG Maker.
